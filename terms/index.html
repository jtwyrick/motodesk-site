The service

TorqueBench is shop management software for motorcycle and powersports repair shops: work orders, customer and vehicle records, invoicing, and reports. It is operated by JWRE Media LLC, doing business as TorqueBench.

Your data

Your shop owns its data. We store it to provide the service and we will never sell it. You can request an export or deletion at any time through support@gettorquebench.com.

QuickBooks

Connecting QuickBooks is optional. An owner connects the shop's own QuickBooks company, and TorqueBench creates customer, invoice, and payment records from counter activity. You can disconnect at any time.

Payments

TorqueBench records counter payments. It does not process card payments and does not move money.

Accounts

Owners control who on their staff has access. Keep your sign-in credentials private. Use the service lawfully.
