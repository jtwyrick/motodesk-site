The daily grind

The counter runs the shop. So why is the counter a whiteboard?

Independent shops don't lose money on the lift. They lose it at the counter - in the gaps between paper tickets, memory, and good intentions.

The whiteboard is the system

Every open job lives on a board or in a notebook - until someone erases the wrong line or the notebook walks off.

Bikes stall on $12 parts

A machine sits for two weeks because nobody tracked that one part was ordered, backordered, or never ordered at all.

"Is my bike ready?" calls

The phone rings all day with status questions, and every answer means stopping a tech to go look.

Estimates on napkins

Quotes get written fast, approved verbally, and argued about later because there's no record of what was promised.

Balances in your head

Who owes what, which ticket is paid, which one walked out - it's tracked in memory until it isn't.

History in one person's head

What was done to this bike last season, which parts, which jetting - it leaves the shop when a person does.

On purpose

Not included in the pilot

Payments are records only. Customer messages are drafts. Supplier ordering stays outside TorqueBench.

Payments: log-only

TorqueBench records balances and payments you take in the shop. It does not charge cards, take online payments, or touch your money.

Customer messaging: draft-only

Pickup and status reminders are prepared as drafts for your review. Nothing messages your customers by itself.

Supplier ordering

Search the catalog and stage draft purchase orders. TorqueBench does not place supplier orders.

Fair questions

FAQ

Is it really free?
Yes. The 6-8 week pilot costs nothing, we never ask for a card, and there's no obligation to continue. Pilot shops get the software, setup, and data imports free with practical feedback when you can.
Do I have to replace my current system?
No. TorqueBench runs alongside whatever you use today - paper, a whiteboard, spreadsheets, or other software. Your current process stays primary for the whole pilot.
What happens to my shop's data?
It's your data. We use it only to run your pilot workspace - never sold, never shared, never used to train anything. You can request an export or deletion at any time.
Does it take payments or message my customers?
No - deliberately. Payments are log-only (you record what you took, nothing is charged), customer reminders are drafts you approve, and supplier ordering stays the way you do it today. There are no automatic customer sends or live supplier orders.
What kinds of shops is TorqueBench for?
Independent motorcycle and powersports repair shops: one counter, a few techs, too many bikes to track on a whiteboard. Cruisers, sport, dirt, scooters, ATVs, vintage - the pilot runs on the motorcycle vertical today.
What does it cost after the pilot?
Pricing is not set yet. Pilot shops get a first look at pricing with no obligation. The pilot itself costs nothing, ever.
Who is building this?
TorqueBench is built in Austin, Texas for independent motorcycle and powersports shops. The pilot exists so the product gets shaped by real counters, not guesses.
