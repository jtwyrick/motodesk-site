Updates

What has changed in TorqueBench, newest first. Only changes a shop can see or use are listed here.

Oct 5, 2026

Faster tickets, fewer taps

Every ticket now has one big button at the top for the next step: record the customer's answer on an estimate, start work, mark ready for pickup, log the payment, or close the ticket. Opening a new work order is quicker too. Type a name or phone to find the customer, add the bike and the complaint, and you're done. VIN, plate, mileage, bay and the rest are tucked under More details.

Oct 5, 2026

Easier on a phone, faster at the counter

The front counter now has a big New work order button. On a ticket, a row of shortcuts (Add line, Payment, Status, Print) jumps you to the right spot and stays in reach as you scroll. When a ticket is ready, the payment amount is already filled in with the balance due. On phones, buttons and links are bigger and easier to tap, the menu keeps its place when you move between pages, and the customer list is easier to read.

Oct 5, 2026

Inventory counts on tickets

Parts you pick from inventory now come off your shelf count when you add the line, estimates included. Void the line or the whole work order and they go back. Receiving stock and setting a count on the Parts page are saved safely even if two people work at once. Low and out-of-stock tags show on the ticket. A sale is never blocked.

Oct 4, 2026

Help link, Contact support form and invite emails

There is a new "help" link in the top bar. It opens a short Contact support form (subject and message). Your message goes to the TorqueBench team with your shop name and email attached, and our reply comes straight to you.

Inviting someone to your shop now emails them the link automatically. The invite button now reads "Send invite", and the join page got the same workbench look as the rest of the app.

Fixed: printed invoices now lay out cleanly on Letter and A4.

Oct 4, 2026

Forgot password by email

The sign-in page now has a "Forgot your password?" link. Enter your email and, if it matches an account, we send a reset link. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes.

The page gives the same answer whether or not the email has an account.

Oct 4, 2026

Setup links by email

New staff logins can now get their setup link by email. They open it once to choose their own password.

Oct 3, 2026

Import customers and parts yourself

Under Admin, Import data, you can upload a CSV file or paste rows for customers or parts. You see a preview before anything is saved. Bad rows are listed and skipped, and existing records are never overwritten.

Oct 3, 2026

Labor rate, address and phone in shop settings

Shop settings now hold a default labor rate, your shop address and your phone number. Printed estimates and invoices show the address and phone.

Oct 3, 2026

Owner setup links

New owners now get a single-use setup link, valid for seven days, to choose their own password.

Oct 2, 2026

Change your password

Every user can now change their own password. A login that was given a temporary password is asked to change it at first sign-in.
