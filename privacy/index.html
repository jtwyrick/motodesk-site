Privacy policy

TorqueBench is shop management software for motorcycle and powersports repair shops. This policy covers the TorqueBench app and website.

Who we are

TorqueBench is operated by JWRE Media LLC, doing business as TorqueBench, a Texas company. Privacy contact: support@gettorquebench.com.

What we store

The records your shop enters: customers, vehicles, work orders, invoices, payments logged at the counter, appointments, parts and supplier details, and photos you attach to work orders. Everything lives in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Render in Ohio, USA.

We store this data to run the service for your shop. We will never sell shop or customer data.

Who receives data

Only the services that run TorqueBench:

  • Render - hosting and database.
  • Google Gmail API - only for email your shop sends through the app.
  • Intuit QuickBooks - only after an owner connects the shop's own QuickBooks company. TorqueBench then creates customer, invoice, and payment records from counter activity. Connection tokens are stored encrypted, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • GitHub - hosts our code. No customer data is stored there.

What we do not do

No third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the app. No payment card processing - TorqueBench records counter payments as log entries; it does not move money.

Exports and deletion

Ask us any time at support@gettorquebench.com. We will export your shop's records or delete them on request. There is no self-serve deletion today, so requests go through the privacy contact. Deleted data can remain in our provider's backups until those backups age out automatically on the provider's schedule.

One honesty note: today's export covers your core records as CSV files. Photos attached to work orders are not yet included in the export - closing that gap is on our list before launch.

Security

All traffic runs over HTTPS. QuickBooks connection tokens are encrypted at rest. Superseded QuickBooks credentials are kept encrypted only so they can be revoked with Intuit.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page.
